Application modules

Every feature in Smart School

This is the full set of modules available after login. Super Admin sees the complete menu. Other roles see the parts that match their work.

Open the application

Front Office

Receive visitors and new families at the front desk.

  • Admission Enquiry with class, source, status and follow-up
  • Visitor Book, Phone Call Log, Postal Dispatch and Receive
  • Complaints and Front Office setup

Student Information

Keep every student record in one place.

  • Student Details search by class, section or keyword
  • Student Admission, Online Admission and Multi Class Student
  • Disabled Students, Categories, House and Disable Reason

Fees Collection

Charge, collect and track school fees.

  • Collect Fees, Quick Fees and Offline Bank Payments
  • Fees Master, Group, Type, Discount and Carry Forward
  • Search payment, due fees and reminders

Academics

Build the school structure used everywhere else.

  • Class and Section (this is the class table used in dropdowns)
  • Subjects, Subject Group and Class Timetable
  • Teachers Timetable, Assign Class Teacher and Promote Students

Attendance

Mark who is present and approve leave.

  • Student Attendance and Attendance By Date
  • Approve Leave for students
  • QR Code Attendance for faster daily marking

Lesson Plan

Plan teaching and track syllabus progress.

  • Manage Lesson Plan on the calendar
  • Manage Syllabus Status by class, section and subject
  • Lesson, Topic and Copy Old Lessons

Examinations

Run school exams and CBSE exams.

  • Exam Group, Schedule, Result, Admit Card and Marksheet
  • Marks Grade and Marks Division
  • CBSE Exam, Assessment, Term, Grade, Observation and Reports

Online Examination & Courses

Deliver digital learning and tests.

  • Online Exam and Question Bank
  • Online Course, Course Category, Certificate Template
  • Offline payment and course purchase reports

Homework & Behaviour

Assign work and record student behaviour.

  • Add Homework and Daily Assignment
  • Assign Incident, Incidents and Behaviour Reports
  • Behaviour settings for points and comments

Human Resource

Manage staff, payroll and leave.

  • Staff Directory, Attendance and Disabled Staff
  • Payroll, Apply Leave, Approve Leave and Leave Type
  • Department, Designation and Teachers Rating

Income, Expenses & Inventory

Track money that is not student fees.

  • Add / Search Income and Income Head
  • Add / Search Expense and Expense Head
  • Items, Stock, Issue Item, Store, Category and Supplier

Library, Transport & Hostel

Run books, buses and boarding.

  • Book List, Import Book, Issue-Return, Student and Staff members
  • Routes, Vehicles, Pickup Points and Transport Fees
  • Hostel, Room Type and Hostel Rooms

Communicate

Reach staff, students and parents.

  • Notice Board, Send Email, Send SMS and logs
  • Login Credentials Send, Email and SMS templates
  • In-app Chat with per-user unread notifications

Certificates, Download Center & Student CV

Print IDs and share files.

  • Student / Staff ID Card, Transfer Certificate and Generate Certificate
  • Upload/Share Content, Video Tutorial and Content Type
  • Build CV and Download CV

Live Classes, CMS & Alumni

Meet online and publish the school website.

  • Google Meet and Zoom live classes and meetings
  • Front CMS pages, news, gallery, events, menus and banners
  • Manage Alumni and Alumni Events

Reports, Multi Branch & System Setting

See the whole school and configure it.

  • Student, Finance, Attendance, Exam, Library, Transport and more reports
  • Multi Branch overview, report and setting
  • General Setting, Session, Roles, Theme, Language, Backup and Notifications

Student & Parent portals

Families use a separate login from staff.

  • Student login for homework, exams, fees, attendance and profile
  • Parent login to follow linked children
  • Use User Login on the login page, not Admin Login
